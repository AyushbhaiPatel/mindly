# Code of conduct

Synapse is built by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want
participating to be welcoming and productive for all of them.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline contributions, and explain decisions when you do.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository's
GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants who violate this code. Reports are handled confidentially.
